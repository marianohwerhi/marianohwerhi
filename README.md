## About me

I'm a Healthcare Data Analyst and Registered Mental Health Nurse based in
the UK. I spend part of my week on a CAMHS inpatient ward and the other part
building Power BI and SQL dashboards — and I've found that combination is
rarer, and more useful, than it sounds. Most healthcare dashboards are built
by analysts who've never worked a clinical shift; I have, and it changes
what I think a dashboard should actually show a care team first.

My focus is healthcare and operational analytics: treatment effectiveness,
patient outcomes, resource planning, and the kind of KPI reporting that
holds up under a clinical governance review, not just a portfolio review.

## Healthcare background

- **Staff Nurse, CAMHS Inpatient Services** — Kestrel Ward, Abergele, North
  Wales. Evidence-based nursing care in a Child and Adolescent Mental Health
  inpatient setting, with accurate clinical documentation supporting audit
  and governance processes.
- Experience spans **CAMHS, acute mental health, rehabilitation, and crisis
  services**, plus prior roles in psychiatric intensive care and general
  healthcare support.
- Registered with the **Nursing and Midwifery Council (NMC)** and a member
  of the **Royal College of Nursing**.
- Trained in clinical governance, audits, risk assessment, safeguarding, and
  GDPR-aware data handling — the regulatory side of healthcare data that a
  dashboard alone doesn't teach you.

## My data analytics journey

I moved into healthcare analytics because I kept seeing the same gap from
the ward side: clinical teams generate huge amounts of data and rarely get
it back in a form that helps them make faster, safer decisions. That's the
problem I now build dashboards to solve.

- Trained through a structured healthcare analytics programme covering SQL,
  Power BI, Tableau, and data modelling, applied specifically to clinical
  and operational datasets.
- Built a 25-year, 150-country Power BI analysis of global HIV treatment
  effectiveness — my most technically complete project (see below).
- Currently extending that into SQL query work and a UK/NHS open-data
  project, to bring the same rigour to a domestic healthcare dataset.
